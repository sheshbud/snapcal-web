SnapCal Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

SnapCal turns a photo of a schedule into calendar events. This policy explains what happens to your data. We keep it minimal on purpose.

What we collect and why

  • Photos and PDFs you scan. When you scan a document, the image is sent to our processing service and on to an AI provider (Anthropic) that reads the dates out of it. It is used only to extract those dates. We do not store your photos on our servers, and they are not used to train AI models.
  • The calendar events you create. These are stored locally on your device, not on our servers. If you delete the app, they are gone.
  • Google sign-in (optional). If you choose "Add to Google Calendar," you grant SnapCal permission to create events on your calendar. We request only the calendar.events scope (create events), nothing else. The sign-in happens through Google; the access token is used once on our server to add your events and is then discarded. We never store it and never read your existing calendar.
  • A random device identifier. We generate a random ID on your device to prevent abuse and rate-limit scans. It is not linked to your name, email, or any personal account.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email, or password (SnapCal has no login).
  • No location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with advertisers.

Third parties

  • Anthropic processes the photo to read its dates. See Anthropic's privacy terms for how they handle API data.
  • Vercel hosts the app and the processing endpoint and may log standard technical request data (such as IP address) for security and reliability.

Children

SnapCal is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can delete all your data at any time by removing events in the app or uninstalling it. Because data lives on your device, uninstalling removes it.
